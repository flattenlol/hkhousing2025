# HOS 2025 Sales

Vocabulary for the two-court sales dashboard.

## Language

**Priority-scheme quota (優先計劃尚餘配額)**:
The court-wide number of flats still available for selection by applicants in the Families with Elderly Members and/or Families with Newborns priority schemes. It is not the count of unsold flats in that court.
_Avoid_: Total remaining flats

**Remaining flat (尚餘單位)**:
A flat marked available in a court block's official sales-status table, regardless of whether a particular applicant category still has quota. A selected or sold flat is not remaining.
_Avoid_: Remaining quota

**Flat status (單位狀態)**:
The official table's classification of a flat as available, selected, or sold. Only available flats have a saleable-area category.

**Saleable-area category (實用面積類別)**:
One of the size bands in the legend of a block's official sales-status PDF. Bands can have different numerical ranges in different blocks.

**Selection-order code (選樓次序代號)**:
The applicant group prefix assigned in the ballot ordering: CM/CS are clearance-affected family/one-person applicants; GP/WP are green-/white-form priority-scheme families; GM/WM are other green-/white-form families; GS/WS are green-/white-form one-person applicants. Detailed eligibility review determines final selection priority, not the prefix alone.

**Confirmed selection slot (已確認選樓時段)**:
The selection date and time communicated to the applicant, distinct from an estimate based on call-number progress.

**Extra ballot number (額外抽籤號碼 R)**:
An additional ballot number for an applicant who unsuccessfully applied to both HOS 2023 and HOS 2024, subject to the scheme's application and eligibility rules. The earlier-ranked ballot number determines selection order.
